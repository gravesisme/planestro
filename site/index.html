MEET PLANESTRO

A local tool for a solo developer coordinating coding agents. Claude or Codex can plan, orchestrate, and implement. Claude is the default. Planestro manages the steps between them, with your backlog, plans, and decisions saved in Markdown.

THE WORK, IN VIEW

Your next decision.
Not another terminal to watch.

See what’s running, what’s ready, and what needs your judgment. Open the plan, ask for a change, or let approved work move forward.

01 / PARALLEL WORK

A worktree for every item.

Agents work on separate branches. Dependencies keep related work in order; epics bring it together on an integration branch.

02 / DURABLE STATE

Your files are the memory.

Plans, notes, and activity live in a Git-versioned data directory. Decisions survive restarts and replacement sessions.

03 / HUMAN JUDGMENT

Answer when you’re ready.

A question parks the session and saves the request. Return to a plan, permission, or review when it has your attention.

04 / CONTROL

Try it. Pause it. Take over.

Start with a dry run. Pause new automation, hold a PR for review, or finish an item yourself. Cleanup requires evidence.
